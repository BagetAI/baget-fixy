## 2. Directory Content Strategy (Fact Cards)

Each category in the Fixy directory now includes a "Fact Card" featuring 5 verified, 2-4yo appropriate facts designed to answer the inevitable "Why?" questions.

### Sample Fact Cards (Categories 11-20)
- **Recycling**: 1. Your blue bin is for paper, plastic, and cans. 2. Old glass bottles can be melted to make brand-new ones. 3. Recycling one tin can saves enough energy to run a TV for 3 hours. 4. Sorting machines use giant magnets to pick up metal. 5. Recycled paper can become a new book!
- **Tractors**: 1. Tractors have huge back tires to help them drive through muddy fields. 2. Some tractors have a special seat called a 'cab' that keeps the farmer dry. 3. They drive very slowly so they can pull heavy plows. 4. One tractor can do the work of 50 horses! 5. The big wheels can be taller than a grown-up.
- **Human Heart**: 1. Your heart is about the same size as your fist. 2. It makes a 'thump-thump' sound because it’s a tiny pump. 3. When you run fast, your heart beats faster to help you move. 4. Your heart never takes a nap—it works even when you sleep! 5. It sends blood to your fingers and your toes.
- **Endangered Species**: 1. Giant Pandas spend 12 hours a day eating bamboo. 2. Sea Turtles can live to be 100 years old. 3. Every tiger has a different pattern of stripes—just like your fingerprints. 4. Snow Leopards use their long tails like a scarf to stay warm. 5. People work in special zoos to keep these animals safe.
- **Baking Science**: 1. Yeast is a tiny living thing that blows bubbles into bread to make it rise. 2. When butter melts, it turns from a block into a yellow puddle. 3. Cookies get brown and crunchy because the oven heat changes the sugar. 4. Flour is made from crushed-up wheat seeds. 5. You can use a kitchen timer to hear exactly when the baking is done!
- **World Maps**: 1. The blue parts of the map are giant oceans filled with water. 2. There are 7 big pieces of land called continents. 3. A compass helps you find which way is North or South. 4. People live in different houses all over the world. 5. You can find where you live on a map by looking for your city!
- **Airport Logistics**: 1. Every suitcase gets a special sticker so it knows which plane to go on. 2. Pilots sit in the 'cockpit' at the very front of the plane. 3. The runway has bright lights to show planes where to land at night. 4. Luggage belts spin in a circle to bring bags to their families. 5. The control tower is like a giant birdhouse that watches all the planes.
- **Deep Sea Fish**: 1. Some fish have a tiny 'light bulb' on their heads to see in the dark. 2. It is very cold and dark at the bottom of the ocean. 3. Deep-sea fish have big eyes to catch any tiny bit of light. 4. Some fish can glow in the dark, just like a glow-stick! 5. They have thick skin to stay safe in the heavy water.
- **Castle Life**: 1. Castles have drawbridges that lift up like a giant door. 2. The walls are made of thick stone to keep it cool inside. 3. Knights wore metal armor that clanked when they walked. 4. Moats are circles of water around a castle to keep it safe. 5. High towers let people see for miles and miles.
- **Musical Percussion**: 1. Drums make noise when you hit them with a stick or your hand. 2. A xylophone has colorful bars that sound like 'ding-ding-ding'. 3. Shakers are filled with tiny beads that rattle when you dance. 4. Every drum has a different 'voice'—some are deep, some are high. 5. You can use your own heart-beat to find a rhythm!
